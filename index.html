Visit the FAQ ↗

Can it fix Apple Photos directly?

No. It repairs exported image files on Windows. It does not access or modify your Apple Photos library.

What if a photo has no date?

The app checks sidecar data, filenames, image timestamp overlays, and chronological context. If no reliable clue exists, you can enter a date manually.

Will moving a photo change its “date taken”?

Usually the embedded capture date is separate from the file's creation date. The app compares them and lets you choose what to repair.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The app is designed to analyze and repair files locally on your Windows PC.
